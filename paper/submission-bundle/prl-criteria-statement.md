# PRL submission form — "Why your paper belongs in PRL" (≤ 100 words)

Thermal diodes have required nonlinearity or structural asymmetry and rectify by at most a factor of ten, independent of size. We show that directional bond dissipation alone turns a reciprocal chain into a heat diode whose rectification ratio obeys an exact law, exp[2(L−1)/ξ], fixed solely by the Liouvillian skin length. The law is independent of energy, temperature and on-site disorder; interactions leave ξ unrenormalised; a thermal bath closes the entropy balance. Extending the Liouvillian skin effect [PRL 123, 170401; 127, 070402; 129, 070401] to heat transport, it gives experiments a size-resolved, falsifiable signature and a design rule for dissipation-engineered heat diodes.
